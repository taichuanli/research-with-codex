## Common causes of failure

1. [agent_inference] The seeds often began with a real software-agent tension but promoted a mature imported object as the contribution: recoverability became reachability, routing became VOI, memory audit became removal attribution and belief update, reversibility became transactional control, and local process credit became standard counterfactual credit. Source: reports/prior-art-cross-field-attack-report.md / Decision audit and Application-transfer eliminations; the corresponding ledgers / transfer_assessment.
2. [agent_inference] Several residuals depended on an exact combination being unoccupied rather than on a distinct problem or mechanism: a four-entry rescue vector, cross-scaffold sign filter, action-conditional rescue label, or executable-feedback remasking loop. Source: reports/prior-art-direct-attack-report.md / Candidate-by-candidate attack; the killed ledgers / novelty_delta and reviewer_rejection.
3. [agent_inference] Code was usually an auditable benchmark rather than a non-substitutable structure. Checkpoints, versioning, noisy observations, costs, provenance, rollback, and hidden rewards can all be reproduced in sufficiently instrumented transactional environments. Source: reports/prior-art-cross-field-attack-report.md / transfer assessments; directions/direction-narrowing-comparison.md / What executable code structure contributes.
4. [agent_inference] The portfolio claimed methods or general control laws before establishing a code-specific empirical phenomenon. After mature mechanisms were removed, the remainder was commonly an applicability study, benchmark, or component ablation. Source: the ten prior-art decisions; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Sections 1, 5, and 6.
5. [agent_inference] Key labels were treated as intrinsic despite being policy-, budget-, action-, or scaffold-dependent: rescue margin depended on the continuation policy, memory necessity on item interactions, diagnosis on a non-unique explanatory vocabulary, and process credit on scaffold serialization. Source: recoverability, memory, diagnosis, and credit ledgers / unverified_information and maximum risks; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Sections 2-3.
6. [agent_inference] The finalist confused the total effect of externally supplied assistance with the endogenous cause of failure. Evidence, state, action, and validation substitutions changed overlapping downstream information and transitions and did not identify an original bottleneck. Source: reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Core claim audit and Interface identifiability audit.
7. [agent_inference] Cross-scaffold language was stronger than the construct. E and V shared observation carriers, S and A shared assistant/action events, and their payloads differed in information source, timing, endpoint proximity, and action cost. Source: reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Sections 2-3.
8. [agent_inference] Endpoint validity was not independent enough to support intermediate causal claims. Hidden tests, developer patches, formal encodings, and reviewer adjudication each remained incomplete for underspecified intent and alternative valid fixes. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P7; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Section 4.
9. [agent_inference] Feasibility gates and scientific support were conflated. The finalist's revised boundary could kill restoration, contract, leak, and gross-effect failures, but its own pass condition established no contribution, novelty, causation, or transfer. Source: directions/minimum-kill-pilot.md / FEASIBILITY_ONLY_PASS; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Section 7.
10. [agent_inference] Shared instrumentation was mistaken for a shared contribution. The rejected umbrella joined stagewise assistance and item-level memory attribution without one estimand, mediator, or positive-result interpretation. Source: directions/direction-narrowing-comparison.md / Unified code-agent causal trajectory auditing.

## Anti-patterns for later idea seeding

1. [agent_inference] Reject a seed whose headline is a software renaming of Q-value, reachability, VOI, active sensing, removal attribution, truth maintenance, reward hacking, robustness, or generic credit assignment. Source: cross-field kill decisions.
2. [agent_inference] Reject exact-combination novelty: adding one label, one control, one feedback source, one matrix row, or one scaffold filter to occupied components is not a contribution by itself. Source: direct-attack kill rationales.
3. [agent_inference] Reject code-as-convenient-dataset arguments. State the program-semantic or repository-version property that changes what can be observed or concluded, and close the seed if a generic transactional simulator preserves the whole question. Source: cross-field transfer assessments.
4. [agent_inference] Do not infer an endogenous failure cause from rescue by an oracle, expert action, corrected message, or later validator. Source: initial independent audit / Interface identifiability audit.
5. [agent_inference] Do not elevate scaffold fields into a universal ontology without a stable semantic mapping and support analysis. Source: re-audit Sections 2 and 8.
6. [agent_inference] Do not call a policy-conditioned or budget-conditioned label an intrinsic state property. Source: recoverability and counterfactual-credit ledgers.
7. [agent_inference] Do not equate passing tests, builds, proofs, hidden validators, or developer-patch similarity with user intent; preserve alternative-valid and inconclusive outcomes. Source: cross-paper synthesis P7; re-audit Section 4.
8. [agent_inference] Do not use transfer as the novelty delta when the only operation is filtering or normalizing labels across scaffolds. Source: scaffold-invariant credit ledger / decision; SEAlign transfer evidence in cross-paper synthesis P9.
9. [agent_inference] Do not propose a controller or training method before observing the claimed error mechanism under independent labels. Source: memory, latent-representation, and diffusion kill rationales.
10. [agent_inference] Do not treat a feasible replay harness or a kill-only screen as evidence that a publishable scientific claim can be supported. Source: re-audit Section 7.
11. [agent_inference] Do not merge candidates merely because they share checkpoints, provenance, validators, or replay infrastructure; require one estimand and one coherent positive and negative result. Source: direction narrowing / Unified code-agent causal trajectory auditing.
12. [agent_inference] Do not treat failure to find an exact predecessor as novelty evidence, especially for recent 2026 preprints with immature citation graphs. Source: AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims; all prior-art ledgers / confidence and unverified_information.

## Finalist failure chain

- [agent_inference] The initial finalist survived only as new empirical causal evidence about heterogeneous rescue profiles, after causal methods, failure taxonomies, recovery windows, and replay infrastructure were excluded from its novelty boundary. Source: directions/direction-narrowing-comparison.md / Stagewise failure attribution; directions/novelty-boundary.md / Occupied space.
- [agent_inference] The first independent audit showed that the substitutions measured overlapping assistance effects rather than endogenous stages and required a single conditional effect, native contracts, untouched replay, leak controls, intent adjudication, stronger baselines, and either adequate transfer scale or removal of transfer. Source: reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Decision and Required revisions before any experiment.
- [agent_inference] The revision honestly removed bottleneck, root-cause, stage, taxonomy, and transfer claims, but this also removed the part that made the direction scientifically distinctive. Source: directions/direction-revision.md / Claims removed or narrowed; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Decision.
- [agent_inference] The re-audit then failed all three gates: the contribution collapsed to assistance benchmarking, the four roles were not a comparable cross-scaffold construct, and the feasibility boundary could not support the remaining claim. Source: directions/independent-direction-reaudit-decision-package.md / Mandatory pass-gate audit.

## Retrospective conclusion

- [agent_inference] The portfolio did not fail because the underlying reliability problems were unimportant. It failed because it converted broad pressure points into methods and causal labels before isolating a code-specific observable phenomenon that survives mature RL, causal inference, information acquisition, robustness, testing, and provenance concepts. Source: common causes above; retained-pressure-point judgment in reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md.
- [agent_inference] The next seeding round must begin from independently checkable evidence deficits and mechanism questions, not from a desired controller, estimator, training objective, or four-stage decomposition. Source: anti-patterns above and requested PRESSURE_POINT_REFRAMING goal.
