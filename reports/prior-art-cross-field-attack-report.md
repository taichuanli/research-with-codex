## IDEA_STAGEWISE_FAILURE_ATTRIBUTION

1. `[agent_inference]` Adjacent names: interchange intervention or mechanism replacement, causal mediation and path-specific effects, actual causality or responsibility attribution in sequential decision processes, and counterfactual credit assignment. Source: [Geiger et al. 2025](https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html), Sections 2.5 and 3.4.1; [Triantafyllou et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1145/3514094.3534133), Sections 2.2-3; [Schug et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16803v2), Sections 3-4.
2. `[direct_evidence]` Mathematical forms and evaluation protocols are mature. Geiger et al. define simple, recursive, and distributed interchange interventions, interchange-intervention accuracy, and total/direct/indirect effects; Triantafyllou et al. map Dec-POMDP trajectories to SCMs and evaluate actual causes in Team Goofspiel; COCOA evaluates counterfactual action contribution against dynamic-programming ground truth on long-horizon tasks; longitudinal mediation gives explicit effect formulas and identification assumptions. Source: Geiger et al., Definitions 44-48 and 52; Triantafyllou et al., Sections 2.2, 3, and 5; Schug et al., Sections 3-5; [Bind et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/biostatistics/kxv029), Sections 2-5.
3. `[agent_inference]` The four-stage replacement mechanism is a direct transfer of interchange intervention plus sequential attribution. A rescue vector is not a new causal estimator, identification result, or standard algorithm. Source: the cited definitions; stagewise ledger / `cross_field_attack.maturity` and `transfer_assessment.direct_transfer`.
4. `[agent_inference]` Code contributes only a narrower test bed with versioned repository and tool state, injectable evidence and patches, and a broader hidden validator. These expose evidence, explicit diagnosis/state, executable action, and acceptance as external interfaces; replay by itself is substitutable by any instrumented simulator, and hidden tests remain an incomplete intent oracle. Source: `synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P2, P5, and P7`; stagewise ledger / `transfer_assessment.code_specific_structure`.
5. `[agent_inference]` Remaining contribution class: `new_causal_evidence`, conditionally `new_phenomenon`. The exact delta is evidence that observationally matching code-agent loops have stable, cross-scaffold rescue vectors over those four executable interfaces. No new problem definition or mechanism remains. Source: stagewise ledger / `transfer_assessment.residual_contribution` and `decision.exact_delta`.
6. `[agent_inference]` A minimum pilot remains direction-killing: 30-50 deterministically restorable failures from two scaffolds, four independently validated substitutions at the same checkpoints, and held-out comparison with trace labels. Kill on unstable restoration, non-separable effects, unresolved intent outcomes, or no predictive gain over ordinary trajectory features. Source: stagewise ledger / `minimum_pilot`.

- `[agent_inference]` Verdict: `NARROW`, medium confidence. Causal machinery is covered; the exact externally typed four-interface phenomenon and its stage-isolation validity remain unverified. Source: stagewise ledger / `cross_field_attack.decision`.

## IDEA_ACTIONABLE_RECOVERABILITY_CALIBRATION

1. `[agent_inference]` Adjacent names: returnability or reset value, probability of rescue, reach-avoid or backward reachability value, recovery or safe set, controllability, robust controlled invariance, viability, and discriminating kernel. Source: [Moldovan and Abbeel 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4810v3), Section 3.2; [Eysenbach et al. 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.06782v1), Section 4.1; [Fisac et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2018.2876389), Definitions 1-4.
2. `[direct_evidence]` Mathematical forms and algorithms are mature. Moldovan and Abbeel define delta-safety by existence of a recall policy that returns home with threshold probability and prove exact feasibility NP-hard; *Leave No Trace* thresholds `Q_reset(s,a)` before irreversible actions; Recovery RL estimates fixed-policy future violation probability and switches over safe/recovery sets; HJ reachability computes invariant safe sets and policies. Source: Moldovan and Abbeel, Equation 3, Theorem 1, and Algorithm 1; Eysenbach et al., Equation 1 and Algorithm 1; [Thananjeyan et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15920v2), Equations III.1 and IV.1-IV.4; Fisac et al., Equations 6-7.
3. `[agent_inference]` Under a fixed rescue policy, binary rescue reward makes the proposed post-action quantity a policy Q-value; under admissible policies it is a reachability or viability value. Separating it from the task reward does not make a new estimand. Source: recoverability ledger / `cross_field_attack.maturity.mathematical_form` and closest-work equations.
4. `[agent_inference]` Code offers cheap snapshots, tests, diffs, and some externally irreversible actions, but those only instantiate the transition system, target set, horizon, auxiliary reward, and policy. Grid worlds, robots, and control systems can substitute for every claimed structural element. Source: all four closest-work experiments; recoverability ledger / `transfer_assessment`.
5. `[agent_inference]` Remaining contribution class: `only_application_transfer`. A positive result would show that an established reset/reachability target is useful for code agents, not define a new phenomenon, problem, mechanism, causal result, or boundary. Source: recoverability ledger / `transfer_assessment.residual_contribution`.
6. `[agent_inference]` A pilot can still kill implementation feasibility if labels vary across reasonable rescue policies or add nothing beyond terminal and task Q-values, but a positive result cannot restore a standalone cross-field delta. Source: recoverability ledger / `minimum_pilot`.

- `[agent_inference]` Verdict: `KILL`, high confidence. The main unverified issues are code-agent belief-state construction and incomplete intent oracles; both are estimation choices inside the covered formulation. Source: recoverability ledger / `cross_field_attack.decision`.

## IDEA_DECISION_VALUE_EVIDENCE_ROUTING

1. `[agent_inference]` Adjacent names: decision-theoretic value of information, Bayesian experimental design, active sensing or sensor management, adaptive or costly feature acquisition, conditional observation planning, and POMDP observation control. Source: [Krause and Guestrin 2009](https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.2737), Section 2; [Janisch et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33013959), Problem Definition; [Spaan and Lima 2009](https://doi.org/10.1609/icaps.v19i1.13381), POMDP model.
2. `[direct_evidence]` The standard VOI form already chooses observations by maximum expected utility of the best action after conditioning, not only entropy reduction; it includes adaptive plans, acquisition penalties, and budgets. Standard algorithms include VOI dynamic programming, acquire-or-decide Q-learning, and belief-state POMDP sensing. Evaluations use utility-cost or accuracy-cost frontiers on sensor, NLP, bioinformatics, eight costly-feature datasets, and a physical ten-camera network. Source: Krause and Guestrin, Equations 1-2 and Sections 2.2, 4, 6-7; Janisch et al., Equations 1-5, Figure 2, and evaluation tables; Spaan and Lima, Tables 1-2 and Figure 4.
3. `[agent_inference]` Paired access and withholding empirically estimate realized VOI. Asking whether the observation changes the argmax action and improves utility is the canonical decision-theoretic definition, while reliability is an observation model and replay or call cost is an acquisition cost. Source: Krause and Guestrin, decision-theoretic VOI subsection; Spaan and Lima, observation model; evidence-routing ledger / `transfer_assessment.direct_transfer`.
4. `[agent_inference]` Code offers typed tool outputs, observable flakiness, inspectable edits, and hidden validation, but these map directly to sensing actions, noisy observations, downstream actions, and utility. Medical, robotics, web-agent, and sensor settings can substitute without changing the decision problem. Source: closest-work domains and evidence-routing ledger / `transfer_assessment`.
5. `[agent_inference]` Remaining contribution class: `only_application_transfer`. At most the proposal creates an auditable code-agent VOI benchmark; it leaves no new formal problem, mechanism, causal estimand, phenomenon, or code-only boundary. Source: evidence-routing ledger / `transfer_assessment.residual_contribution`.
6. `[agent_inference]` A 50-100 checkpoint pilot can kill feasibility if paired effects are unstable or standard VOI matches the proposed labels, but positive performance would remain a domain implementation. Source: evidence-routing ledger / `minimum_pilot`.

- `[agent_inference]` Verdict: `KILL`, high confidence. Stochastic language-agent label sample complexity and an exhaustive audit of every 2026 web-agent preprint remain unverified, but mature pre-LLM theory already covers the claimed principle. Source: evidence-routing ledger / `cross_field_attack.decision`.

## IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY

1. `[agent_inference]` Adjacent names: removal- or perturbation-based attribution, leave-one-out and occlusion, necessity and sufficiency, comprehensiveness and sufficiency, context or data attribution, poisoning robustness, and symbolically belief contraction or revision and truth maintenance. Source: [Covert et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14878v2), Definitions 1-3; [DeYoung et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.408), Section 4.2; [Balkir et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2022.naacl-main.192), Sections 3-4; [ContextCite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00729v2), Section 2.
2. `[direct_evidence]` Mathematical forms and evaluation protocols are mature. Covert et al. formalize subset functions, removal/inclusion effects, Shapley contributions, and high/low-value subsets; ERASER defines deletion-based comprehensiveness and retain-only sufficiency across seven datasets; Balkir et al. use generative plausible replacements for causal necessity/sufficiency; ContextCite uses top-k and random context ablations, LDS, a sparse surrogate, pruning, and poison detection. Source: Covert et al., Definitions 1-3, Table 1, and Sections 4-6; DeYoung et al., Equations 1-3; Balkir et al., Sections 3-5; ContextCite, Definitions 2.2-2.3, Algorithm 1, and Sections 4-5.
3. `[agent_inference]` Generic per-item remove, corrupt, and restore is a direct combination of mature removal attribution, necessity/sufficiency, and robustness testing. It cannot support a new generic audit or memory-control mechanism. Source: memory ledger / `cross_field_attack.maturity` and `transfer_assessment.direct_transfer`.
4. `[agent_inference]` The only potentially non-substitutable code structure is snapshot-specific executable truth: a remembered tool result, edit claim, or negative finding can carry repository-version provenance, be rechecked against current code, and be scored at both the next executable action and hidden behavioral outcome. Generic language contexts support the same ablations, so this distinction survives only if version grounding changes audit validity. Source: `synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P3-P4 and P7`; ContextCite Appendix C.4; memory ledger / `transfer_assessment`.
5. `[agent_inference]` Remaining contribution class: `new_applicability_boundary`, conditionally `new_causal_evidence`. The delta is not a new attribution method; it is whether deletion-based attribution remains valid under evolving executable state and whether version-stale retention versus useful deletion produces action and hidden-outcome effects missed by response-probability attribution. Source: memory ledger / `transfer_assessment.residual_contribution` and `decision.exact_delta`.
6. `[agent_inference]` A minimum pilot remains direction-killing: 30-50 restorable checkpoints, provenance-linked facts whose truth changes across repository versions, exact remove/valid restore/program-false corruption, and matched-token measurement at action and hidden-test levels. Kill on unverifiable item truth, deletion artifacts, dominant interactions, no extra signal beyond response probability, or equivalence to ContextCite-style attribution. Source: memory ledger / `minimum_pilot`.

- `[agent_inference]` Verdict: `NARROW`, medium confidence. Generic audit coverage is strong; the exact version-grounded two-endpoint validity boundary was not found and may still collapse into application transfer. Source: memory ledger / `cross_field_attack.decision`.
