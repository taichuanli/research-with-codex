## Re-verification of the four pressure points

### REPRODUCIBLE_EVIDENCE_BEFORE_STATE

- Already covered: [direct_evidence] APR Traceability quantifies reproducer failure, IssueExec quantifies uncovered ground-truth functions, EvidenT reports unusable or inconsistent evidence, AgentCheck perturbs recorded tool responses, and Datura studies manipulation of tool outputs and metadata. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Failure and recovery chain Stage 1 and P2; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Section 6; AgentCheck at https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11098; Datura official record cited in the re-audit.
- Mature generic coverage: [agent_inference] Noisy observation models, robustness and fault injection, data provenance, measurement error, active sensing, and decision-theoretic VOI already cover reliability, acquisition, cost, and downstream utility at the abstract level. Source: ideas/prior-art/IDEA_DECISION_VALUE_EVIDENCE_ROUTING.yaml / cross_field_attack.maturity and transfer_assessment; ideas/prior-art/IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY.yaml / direction_narrowing_targeted_verification.
- Code-only structure: [agent_inference] The retained structure is an observation whose truth can be regenerated by executing a recorded command, test, build, analyzer, or reproducer against an exact source tree, dirty diff, dependency environment, and tool version, with source-level referents that may change after an edit. If only source authority or generic lineage matters, code is substitutable and this pressure point closes. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P2; directions/direction-revision.md / Evidence contract and provenance firewall.
- Missing observation or mechanism evidence: [agent_inference] The corpus does not establish how often evidence actually consumed by an agent is non-reproducible, stale for the active repository state, semantically mis-scoped, or provenance-incomplete, nor how often later failure is caused by invalid evidence rather than misuse of valid evidence. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Principal evidence weaknesses and P2; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Retained pressure points.
- Disposition: [agent_inference] RETAIN, but only as evidence-validity and provenance questions before state construction. Close any routing, call-selection, recoverability, or generic tool-robustness restatement. Source: VOI and recoverability cross-field kills; PF1 below.

### DERIVED_STATE_FIDELITY

- Already covered: [direct_evidence] Watson demonstrates non-unique reconstructed reasoning, ReduceFix preserves a failure predicate, SpecGuru checks generated summaries, CausalRepair supplies slices, ContextCite performs context ablation, and truth-maintenance and belief-update work covers provenance, dependency, retraction, and changed-world facts. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P3; ideas/prior-art/IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY.yaml / cross_field_attack and direction_narrowing_targeted_verification.
- Mature generic coverage: [agent_inference] Rationale faithfulness, factuality, sufficient statistics, belief states, information bottlenecks, causal representation, removal attribution, truth maintenance, and belief revision already cover the abstract questions of truth, compression, dependence, and update. Source: ideas/prior-art/IDEA_CAUSAL_DIAGNOSIS_ACTION_GATE.yaml / works and decision; ideas/prior-art/IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY.yaml / cross_field_attack.maturity.
- Code-only structure: [agent_inference] A derived claim can refer to a concrete symbol, dependency, path condition, failing behavior, invariant, configuration, or candidate diff at a specific repository revision, and many such claims can be checked by program analysis or execution. Later edits can make a formerly true state stale, while future code actions have inspectable semantic preconditions and side effects. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Stage 2 and P3; direction-narrowing memory analysis / What executable code structure contributes.
- Missing observation or mechanism evidence: [agent_inference] Existing work rarely labels both whether a derived state is true at the revision where it is used and whether it preserves the information necessary for a later correct action. It also rarely separates missing program facts from shorter context, style, wording, or correlated endpoint cues. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Principal evidence weaknesses and P3.
- Disposition: [agent_inference] RETAIN and split into two questions: version-aligned truth in PF2 and future-action information sufficiency in PF3. Do not revive per-item memory control or rationale-invariance gating. Source: memory and diagnosis final decisions; PF2-PF3 below.

### CHECKABLE_ACCEPTANCE_VS_INTENT

- Already covered: [direct_evidence] PatchDiff quantifies false acceptance and persistent ambiguity, To Run shows disagreement between agent-selected validation and official evaluation, formal systems establish only encoding-relative correctness, and CARE plus Nine Judges cover correlated evaluators. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Stage 4, Contradiction 2, and P7; ideas/prior-art/IDEA_ORACLE_CONCURRENCE_TRAP.yaml / decision.
- Mature generic coverage: [agent_inference] The oracle problem, test adequacy, specification gaming, reward hacking, Goodhart effects, ensemble dependence, adversarial validation, and formal specification fidelity already cover the broad gap between a proxy and intended behavior. Source: cross-paper synthesis P7; oracle-concurrence ledger; re-audit Section 4.
- Code-only structure: [agent_inference] The narrow residual exists when the candidate action can also change, select, bypass, weaken, or share assumptions with executable validator artifacts inside the same repository and build graph, including tests, fixtures, mocks, generated checks, configuration, or reachability of validated code. That candidate-validator co-change is observable in repository history and execution. Source: To Run and TestPrune evidence in cross-paper synthesis; AuditRepairBench channel-surgery evidence at https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04624v1.
- Missing observation or mechanism evidence: [agent_inference] The reviewed evidence does not separate false acceptance caused by an incomplete but fixed oracle from false acceptance caused by an agent and its validators becoming wrong in the same internally consistent way. It also does not establish when validator selection or mutation changes the acceptance surface enough to reward an intent-invalid patch. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P7; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Sections 3-4.
- Disposition: [agent_inference] CLOSE the broad intent-oracle and validator-diversity formulations as mature problems; RETAIN only the code-specific candidate-validator co-change question in PF4. Source: oracle-concurrence kill; finalist intent judgment; PF4 below.

## Reframed problem families

### PF1. Evidence provenance and replay validity

- Problem definition: [agent_inference] For an evidence item actually consumed by a code agent, determine whether its claimed observation can be regenerated from recorded provenance at the exact repository and environment state where it informed a decision, and whether the observation refers to the behavior or program entity the agent treats it as evidence about. Source: REPRODUCIBLE_EVIDENCE_BEFORE_STATE re-verification above.
- Why this is not a direct mature-concept application: [agent_inference] Provenance systems record lineage, robustness studies inject faults, and noisy-observation models price reliability, but none of those alone establishes use-time semantic validity of an executed observation against a changing source tree. The family closes if lineage metadata without re-execution and program-reference checking explains the same failures. Source: AgentCheck and provenance-sensitivity coverage in the re-audit; EvidenT and APR Traceability in cross-paper synthesis P2.
- Code environment non-substitutability: [agent_inference] The question requires a source-level program revision, executable command or checker, dependency and process environment, and a mapping from the resulting observation to code entities or behavior. A generic transactional domain is not enough unless it supplies the same executable program-reference relation, in which case no code-exclusive claim is allowed. Source: direction revision / executable checkpoint and evidence contract; cross-field substitutability rule.
- Closest work: [direct_evidence] APR Traceability and EvidenT expose missing, unusable, or inconsistent evidence; AgentCheck records and perturbs tool responses (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11098); Auditing Provenance Sensitivity changes source authority while holding proposition and policy fixed (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20827). Source locators: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P2; reports/independent-direction-reaudit-report.md / Section 6.
- Result that would make the problem unimportant: [agent_inference] The family is unimportant if provenance-complete re-execution shows that invalid, stale, or mis-scoped consumed evidence is rare, has negligible association with later wrong actions after state quality is accounted for, and generic tool-fault or provenance models explain the remaining cases. Source: problem definition and mature-coverage boundary above.
- Contribution fit: [agent_inference] Best fit is a new empirical finding and measurement boundary. A method contribution is premature until a reproducible error mechanism is established; training is not the first contribution. Source: first-round anti-pattern 9.
- Difference from eliminated frames: [agent_inference] It does not estimate ability to recover after an action (recoverability), choose which observation to buy (VOI routing), score retained context items (memory audit), inject correct assistance at one stage (stagewise assistance), or assign endpoint reward to steps (generic credit assignment). Source: reports/pressure-point-failure-retrospective.md / Anti-patterns; corresponding prior-art decisions.

### PF2. Truth of derived state at the active repository revision

- Problem definition: [agent_inference] For an explicit plan, diagnosis, summary, localization, invariant, or working hypothesis used by a code agent, determine which claims are true, false, unsupported, or stale with respect to the exact repository revision and executable environment at the moment the state is consumed. Source: DERIVED_STATE_FIDELITY re-verification above.
- Why this is not a direct mature-concept application: [agent_inference] Rationale faithfulness tests sensitivity to text, truth-maintenance systems track dependency and retraction, and factuality checks compare claims with evidence; the unresolved code question is whether mixed agent state claims have operational truth conditions grounded in source, analysis, and execution rather than in endpoint agreement or explanation plausibility. Source: diagnosis ledger / works and decision; memory ledger / targeted belief-revision and TMS checks; Watson evidence in cross-paper synthesis P3.
- Code environment non-substitutability: [agent_inference] The family is code-bound only for claims about symbols, dependencies, path conditions, tests, failures, side effects, configuration, or candidate diffs that can be checked against a specific program revision. Free-form plans without program-grounded truth conditions belong to generic factuality and are out of scope. Source: cross-paper synthesis P3; direction-narrowing memory code-boundary analysis.
- Closest work: [direct_evidence] Watson finds divergent reconstructed traces for identical answers; ReduceFix preserves a failure predicate; SpecGuru differentially checks summaries; CausalRepair packages program slices; truth-maintenance and belief-update work covers dependency and changed-world facts. Source locators: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P3; ideas/prior-art/IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY.yaml / direction_narrowing_targeted_verification.
- Result that would make the problem unimportant: [agent_inference] The family is unimportant if independently checkable derived claims are usually true and revision-aligned, false or stale claims do not predict downstream action errors beyond generic context quality, or existing factuality and truth-maintenance checks recover the same distinctions. Source: problem and mature-coverage boundary above.
- Contribution fit: [agent_inference] Best fit is an empirical finding about state-truth failure modes, with a possible later measurement method. It is not yet a training contribution. Source: P3 missing-evidence boundary.
- Difference from eliminated frames: [agent_inference] It evaluates the truth status of the constructed state itself, not post-action reachability, observation choice, per-item retention utility, rescue by replacing S with an oracle, or local reward credit. Source: recoverability, VOI, memory, finalist, and credit kill decisions.

### PF3. Information sufficiency for later executable actions

- Problem definition: [agent_inference] Determine whether an agent-created intermediate state preserves the program facts and relations needed to choose among later executable actions, including cases where every retained statement is true but a necessary dependency, constraint, counterexample, or negative finding has been omitted. Source: DERIVED_STATE_FIDELITY re-verification and fundamental-question check above.
- Why this is not a direct mature-concept application: [agent_inference] Belief-state sufficiency and information bottlenecks define the abstract target, while ContextCite and removal attribution measure response sensitivity. They do not establish whether a self-generated state preserves program-semantic prerequisites for a later repository transition under changing code, nor distinguish omission of a necessary relation from token length or style. Source: ideas/prior-art/IDEA_COUNTERFACTUAL_EVIDENCE_MEMORY.yaml / cross_field_attack.maturity and code boundaries; synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P3.
- Code environment non-substitutability: [agent_inference] Later code actions have executable preconditions and effects over files, symbols, dependencies, tests, and builds; a missing relation can be exposed by a concrete failed transition or behavioral counterexample at a known revision. If sufficiency can be judged only by model response probability, the family collapses to generic context attribution and closes. Source: ContextCite boundary in the memory ledger; cross-paper synthesis Stage 3.
- Closest work: [direct_evidence] Plans Don't Persist removes plans under replay and finds that stale plan resurfacing does not restore working state (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22953v1); ContextCite attributes generation to context (https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.00729v2); ERASER defines comprehensiveness and sufficiency (DOI:10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.408); ReduceFix preserves a failure predicate. Source locators: memory prior-art ledger / works and cross_field_attack; cross-paper synthesis P3-P4.
- Result that would make the problem unimportant: [agent_inference] The family is unimportant if truth-aligned intermediate states retain all action-relevant information at practical compression levels, generic context-attribution scores identify every consequential omission, or omitted program relations do not cause action changes after budget and wording are controlled. Source: problem definition and closest-work boundary above.
- Contribution fit: [agent_inference] Best fit is a new empirical finding and potentially a cross-domain mechanism about state sufficiency, but only if executable program relations explain failures beyond generic context attribution. Method or training contributions must wait. Source: application-transfer rule and first-round failure retrospective.
- Difference from eliminated frames: [agent_inference] It asks what information the constructed state must preserve, not whether a state remains recoverable, whether evidence should be acquired, whether a memory item should be retained, whether oracle state assistance rescues a run, or how endpoint reward should be divided. Source: the five eliminated-frame ledgers and finalist audit.

### PF4. Wrong but self-consistent executable acceptance

- Problem definition: [agent_inference] Determine when a code agent reaches executable acceptance because the candidate solution and the validation surface share the same mistaken assumption or because the candidate action changes which behaviors are checked, even though the resulting patch violates independently adjudicated intent. Source: CHECKABLE_ACCEPTANCE_VS_INTENT re-verification above.
- Why this is not a direct mature-concept application: [agent_inference] Test inadequacy, specification gaming, false acceptance, correlated judges, and deceptive feedback are mature. The retained question is narrower: whether repository actions endogenously co-change candidate behavior and executable validation so that wrongness becomes internally consistent, rather than merely exploiting a fixed weak reward. Source: PatchDiff and To Run evidence in cross-paper synthesis P7; AuditRepairBench channel-surgery boundary; oracle-concurrence kill.
- Code environment non-substitutability: [agent_inference] Tests, fixtures, mocks, build configuration, generated specifications, coverage paths, and validation reachability can be versioned executable artifacts in the same repository graph as the candidate. The family closes if the validator is fixed and external or if generic judge manipulation fully explains the outcome. Source: cross-paper synthesis Stage 4; AuditRepairBench at https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04624v1.
- Closest work: [direct_evidence] PatchDiff finds accepted but behaviorally divergent patches and many indeterminate cases (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15223v2); To Run reports agent-selected validation disagreement; AuditRepairBench blocks evaluator-derived selector information; Helpful Agent Meets Deceptive Judge studies misleading critiques (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03332). Source locators: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P7; independent re-audit / Sections 4 and 6.
- Result that would make the problem unimportant: [agent_inference] The family is unimportant if freezing validation outside the candidate repository removes nearly all false acceptance, candidate or validator co-change is not associated with intent-invalid outcomes, and standard test-adequacy or reward-hacking explanations account for the rest. Source: problem and mature-coverage boundary above.
- Contribution fit: [agent_inference] Best fit is an empirical finding, conditionally a cross-domain mechanism for endogenous validation. A new validator or training objective would be premature. Source: first-round method-before-phenomenon failure.
- Difference from eliminated frames: [agent_inference] It concerns how candidate and validator become jointly wrong, not recovery probability, evidence acquisition utility, retained-memory attribution, rescue from a corrected V message, or step credit from endpoint success. Source: cross-field kills and finalist re-audit.

### PF5. Endpoint-trained process fidelity across scaffold encodings

- Problem definition: [agent_inference] For endpoint-derived process labels or policies, determine whether the learned preference tracks an executable repository transition that is semantically useful across scaffolds or instead tracks scaffold-specific transcript fields, action serialization, policy occupancy, or validator exposure. Source: ENDPOINT_CREDIT_PROCESS_FIDELITY_TRANSFER re-verification above.
- Why this is not a direct mature-concept application: [agent_inference] Counterfactual credit estimators, process reward models, and domain-transfer methods are mature. The retained question does not propose another estimator or sign filter; it asks which external semantic object endpoint supervision attaches to when multiple transcript encodings induce the same repository transition, and why apparent process fidelity fails under scaffold change. Source: scaffold-credit ledger / decision; SEAlign evidence in cross-paper synthesis P9.
- Code environment non-substitutability: [agent_inference] Tool calls and reasoning strings can differ while their executed command effects, repository diffs, dependency state, and behavioral outcomes are equivalent and independently inspectable. This permits a semantic transition comparison unavailable from text labels alone. If transcript normalization without repository semantics predicts transfer equally well, close the family as generic representation shift. Source: direction revision / scaffold-specific mappings; re-audit Sections 2 and 5.
- Closest work: [direct_evidence] SEAlign reports OpenHands-to-AutoCodeRover degradation (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18455); CVT-RL uses policy-conditioned counterfactual credit (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05263); C3 uses exact history restoration (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06859); CRAFT uses sibling rollout credit (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29476); CSO verifies outcome-flipping actions (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03412). Source locators: scaffold-credit prior-art ledger / works and decision.
- Result that would make the problem unimportant: [agent_inference] The family is unimportant if process labels remain stable after executable semantic normalization, cross-scaffold transfer matches same-scaffold performance under controlled model and data, or transfer failures are fully explained by ordinary policy or representation shift without a repository-transition effect. Source: problem and generic-coverage boundary above.
- Contribution fit: [agent_inference] Best fit is an empirical mechanism or cross-domain mechanism contribution. A training contribution is justified only after a scaffold-encoding failure mechanism is observed independently of a new credit algorithm. Source: first-round credit kill and P9 missing evidence.
- Difference from eliminated frames: [agent_inference] It does not define recoverability, route evidence, audit memory items, compare oracle assistance stages, or introduce generic counterfactual credit. It evaluates whether already endpoint-derived supervision is faithful to externally equivalent code transitions. Source: the corresponding prior-art decisions and re-audit construct failure.

## Main evidence risks

- [direct_evidence] Seven corpus papers have artifact-plus-abstract evidence and one has only an official abstract; their methods, ablations, and threats cannot be treated as manuscript-level evidence. Source: synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / Principal evidence weaknesses and Evidence-confidence summary.
- [agent_inference] Several decisive competing works are recent 2026 preprints with immature versions, citation graphs, and independent replication. Their direct mechanism coverage is evidence, but their long-term status and boundary remain uncertain. Source: prior-art ledgers / confidence and unverified_information.
- [agent_inference] No complete independent intent oracle exists for underspecified issues and alternative valid fixes, so PF4 outcomes can remain indeterminate rather than valid or invalid. Source: PatchDiff evidence; re-audit Section 4.
- [agent_inference] Replay, provider drift, dependency nondeterminism, and state restoration can invalidate causal or longitudinal comparisons even when repository hashes match. Source: independent audit / Reproducibility and transfer; re-audit / Untouched replay stability.
- [agent_inference] Derived-state truth and sufficiency labels can be non-unique, action-dependent, and interaction-dominated; a single explanation vocabulary or item ablation cannot be assumed causal. Source: diagnosis and memory ledgers / maximum risks and unverified_information.
- [agent_inference] The claim that any retained family is code-specific remains conditional. Versioning alone, provenance alone, or generic transactional replay is insufficient; each family must retain an executable program-semantic relation or be closed as application transfer. Source: cross-field transfer assessments and status rule above.
- [agent_inference] These problem families state missing evidence, not confirmed novelty or importance. A missing exact predecessor and a plausible mechanism are not evidence that the phenomenon is frequent, consequential, or publishable. Source: AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims; first-round failure retrospective.
