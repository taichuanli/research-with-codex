## 1. Revised research question and unique estimand

- `[agent_inference]` Revised research question: At an exactly restored executable code-agent checkpoint, how much does replacing one native, model-visible evidence (`E`), explicit derived-state (`S`), executable-action (`A`), or validation (`V`) carrier with an independently validated and leak-audited assistance change the probability of an intent-valid repair within a fixed continuation budget, relative to untouched replay, and is that change distinguishable from neutral-carrier and mismatched-content effects? Source: audit-required estimand and contrast repairs; `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Interface identifiability audit; Rescue, placebo, untouched replay, and hidden correctness`.
- `[agent_inference]` The target population is only the predeclared distribution of checkpoints that pass restoration, native-carrier, oracle-provenance, and intent-adjudication gates. It is not all code-agent failures, all looping trajectories, or all tasks. Source: audit selection warning in `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Core claim audit / Same surface failure loop`.
- `[agent_inference]` Let `C_i` be the complete restored checkpoint, `G_i` the frozen scaffold/model continuation policy and budget, `Z_i` the outcome in `{valid, invalid, inconclusive}`, `Y_i = 1[Z_i = valid]` the confirmed-valid indicator, and `T_j^o` a valid oracle substitution on interface `j` in `{E,S,A,V}`. The sole estimand family is the checkpoint-conditional intention-to-treat total effect:

  `tau_j(C_i) = P(Y_i = 1 | T_j^o, C_i, G_i) - P(Y_i = 1 | untouched, C_i, G_i)`.

  Source: audit requirement to use oracle versus untouched as the primary effect; `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Required revisions before any experiment / 1-2`.
- `[agent_inference]` `R(C_i) = [tau_E(C_i), tau_S(C_i), tau_A(C_i), tau_V(C_i)]` is a vector-valued summary of the same estimand under four treatment contracts, not four causal-stage estimands. Sham and mismatched contrasts are diagnostics for carrier activation and misleading content; they do not replace the primary estimand. Source: same audit contrast requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` Each `tau_j` is the total downstream effect of externally supplying one assistance through a declared native carrier. Downstream state, actions, repository mutations, observations, and stopping may all change after treatment by design. Source: `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Interface identifiability audit`.
- `[agent_inference]` A large `tau_j`, a largest entry in `R(C_i)`, or rescue after an oracle substitution does not identify the agent's endogenous bottleneck, original error, root cause, mediator, or failure cause. Source: independent audit judgment that the substitutions are overlapping downstream assistances, not identified endogenous stages.

### Executable checkpoint and treatment timing

- `[agent_inference]` `C_i` is the immutable repository/container/process manifest plus the complete active scaffold event prefix after a completed native action-observation pair and immediately before the next policy call. It also records, before outcomes, the exact eligible carrier IDs for E, S, and V. Source: prior executable-checkpoint manifest narrowed by the fixed carrier contract.
- `[agent_inference]` E, S, and V treatments fork `C_i` by replacing only their predeclared model-visible carrier in the copied prefix. E and V must reference different source-attributed observation events; if one event serves both roles, the checkpoint is ineligible. Source: audit E/V fusion warning and contract invariants.
- `[agent_inference]` A treatments fork the first native model response after `C_i`: within each repeat, untouched executes its proposed action, while the A sham, mismatch, and oracle branches preserve that response's visible/private reasoning and replace only the serializer-coupled executable-action value before dispatch. Source: audit action-transition warning and scaffold action schemas.
- `[agent_inference]` All branches therefore originate from the same pre-treatment `C_i`; the different treatment timings are part of the declared interface contracts and do not imply that E/S/A/V are simultaneous or orthogonal mediators. Source: total-effect interpretation.

## 2. Cross-scaffold native contract

### Contract-wide invariants

- `[agent_inference]` A native carrier is a scaffold-defined serialized field or event that is consumed or executed by the unmodified continuation loop. A generic extra prompt invented only for the study is not native. Source: `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Interface identifiability audit`.
- `[agent_inference]` A substitution changes one logical carrier value. Serializer-coupled duplicates, such as a parsed action plus its raw tool-call encoding, may change together only when a round-trip equality audit proves that they encode the same logical value. Source: native event schemas cited below; audit off-target requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` Every branch preserves the repository tree, process/container state, transcript events other than the declared carrier, scaffold configuration, model request configuration, remaining budget, and checkpoint identity until the substituted value is consumed or executed. Source: `directions/minimum-kill-pilot.md / Executable checkpoint` as narrowed by the audit.
- `[agent_inference]` Independence means direct-write isolation at the carrier boundary, not mediator independence: only the declared logical carrier may be directly changed, while all causally downstream variables are allowed to respond. Source: the fixed total-effect estimand above.
- `[agent_inference]` A checkpoint is ineligible if a carrier is absent, hidden from the next policy call, discarded by context processing, fused with another target in a way that cannot be round-tripped, structurally invalid after replacement, or outside the support of the scaffold's ordinary event schema. Source: audit requirement to drop fused or absent interfaces.

## 3. Intervention conditions and primary contrasts

| Condition | Definition | Inferential role |
|---|---|---|
| Untouched replay | `[agent_inference]` Restore `C_i` and run the frozen native continuation without changing a carrier. Source: audit-required primary comparator. | `[agent_inference]` Primary counterfactual baseline for every `tau_j`; it estimates the checkpoint's native continuation distribution. Source: fixed estimand. |
| Neutral sham | `[agent_inference]` Replace the same native carrier using the same schema, source markers, serialization path, length band, and branch budget, but with a preregistered task-agnostic payload that makes no factual, diagnostic, action, or acceptance claim. For `A`, use a reversible validated no-op and retain its ordinary observation. Source: audit-required separate sham. | `[agent_inference]` Diagnostic contrast `P(Y|sham_j)-P(Y|untouched)` measures carrier activation, formatting, authority, and no-op cost; empirical non-neutrality is reported rather than assumed away. Source: audit sham warning. |
| Harmful or mismatched control | `[agent_inference]` Replace the same carrier with a schema-matched payload drawn from a disjoint checkpoint or independently shown false/irrelevant for `C_i`. Action controls must remain local, reversible, and non-destructive; "harmful" never authorizes external side effects. Source: old harmful-control intent narrowed by operational safety. | `[agent_inference]` Diagnostic contrast `P(Y|mismatch_j)-P(Y|untouched)` measures susceptibility to semantically wrong assistance and detects an invalid control if the payload is accidentally valid. Source: audit requirement to separate harmful controls. |
| Oracle substitution | `[agent_inference]` Replace the carrier with the interface-bounded payload that passes provenance, semantic-validity, leak, and intent-firewall checks below. Source: oracle audit requirement. | `[agent_inference]` Primary contrast is oracle versus untouched. Oracle versus sham is secondary evidence that semantic content, rather than carrier activation, matters; oracle versus mismatch is descriptive and cannot inflate the primary effect. - `[agent_inference]` All four conditions use the same post-checkpoint decision, token, tool, and wall-clock policy. A treatment cannot gain extra continuation steps because its carrier consumes a step; any carrier-specific native cost remains part of its intention-to-treat total effect. Source: matched continuation requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` The intervention audit compares event hashes and logical-field diffs before any outcome is revealed. Any undeclared direct change is inconclusive; a systematic serializer coupling not covered by the contract fails that interface. Source: audit off-target requirement.

## 4. Oracle provenance, leak audit, and intent adjudication

### Provenance and information firewall

- `[agent_inference]` Every oracle record must freeze: checkpoint and repository hashes; scaffold commit; interface; payload and serializer hashes; builder identity/role; every input artifact and command; allowed and forbidden information sets; build time; independent semantic reviewer; and post-freeze validation record. Source: audit demand for reproducible oracle provenance.
- `[agent_inference]` `E`, `S`, and `A` builders may access only the public task statement, restored repository, public tests, and observations generated from that state. They may not access the developer patch, hidden tests, hidden expected outputs, other oracle payloads from later interfaces, or future trajectory suffixes. Source: gold-leak risk in `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Rescue, placebo, untouched replay, and hidden correctness`.
- `[agent_inference]` The `V` builder interacts with held-back validators only through a redaction boundary that returns the predeclared bounded verdict/counterexample schema; hidden source, test names, expected values not already public, stack traces that reveal patch location, and solution text are withheld. Source: P7 intent boundary and audit leak requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` The developer patch is used only after treatment payloads are frozen, as a non-unique environment positive control and optional adjudication reference. No treatment is copied or derived from it. Source: audit finding that a developer-patch replay proves only one admissible solution.
- `[agent_inference]` An `A` oracle intentionally contains action semantics; this is treatment content, not permission to use gold. It must be independently authored while blinded and then shown post-freeze to be atomic, executable, and on an intent-valid path. Source: action contract above.

### Leak audit

- `[agent_inference]` Before branching, an automated audit checks literal and normalized overlap with developer-patch hunks, hidden test identifiers/source, hidden expected outputs, future actions, and disallowed file/line or command fields for each interface. Source: audit gold/hidden-information requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` Two reviewers, blind to branch outcomes, separately judge whether the payload exceeds its interface schema or implies a later-stage answer; disagreement is adjudicated before use. Source: independent audit requirement and P5/P7 uncertainty.
- `[agent_inference]` Exact overlap is reported, not treated mechanically as proof of leakage: independently authored valid actions may coincide with a developer edit. Missing provenance, any confirmed forbidden-source access, or unresolved semantic leakage makes the branch inconclusive; repeated leakage for any interface kills that interface contract. Source: evidence-discipline requirement.

### Intent and alternative-patch adjudication

- `[agent_inference]` Outcome adjudication never uses textual equality to the developer patch. A candidate is `valid` only when it satisfies the public issue requirements, passes the predeclared target-behavior oracle family, and introduces no detected regression under the predeclared regression/property family. Source: PatchDiff ambiguity in `cards/ICSE2026_are-solved-issues-in-swe-bench-really-solved-correctly-an-empirical-stud.yaml / evaluation.main_results and failure_modes`.
- `[agent_inference]` Two treatment-blind reviewers assess issue intent and the alternative patch independently; a third reviewer adjudicates disagreement and may consult the developer patch only as a non-unique reference after recording an initial judgment. Source: audit requirement for independent requirement/developer adjudication.
- `[agent_inference]` An alternative patch that meets the intent and behavioral criteria is counted as valid even when its files, algorithm, or diff differ from the developer patch. Source: same alternative-valid-fix boundary.
- `[agent_inference]` Mark `Z` as `inconclusive` when requirements are underspecified, validator families conflict, regression coverage is materially incomplete, reviewers cannot resolve intent, nondeterminism prevents a stable result, or an alternative implementation cannot be distinguished from benchmark overfitting. Retain the branch: it contributes no confirmed-valid event to the conservative primary `Y`, contributes a valid event to a partial-identification upper-bound sensitivity, and is never relabeled `invalid`. Report both bounds and treatment-specific inconclusive rates. Source: audit hidden-correctness and treatment-selection warnings; `AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims`.
- `[agent_inference]` Also mark a branch inconclusive before outcome analysis when restoration fails, a contract is absent/fused, a direct off-target change occurs, oracle provenance is incomplete, leakage is unresolved, a sham carries task semantics, or a mismatched control is accidentally valid. Treatment-concentrated inconclusiveness is a construct failure, not missing-at-random data. Source: audit intervention and selection warnings.

## 6. Residual contribution and falsifiability

- `[agent_inference]` The exact residual contribution is a code-agent applicability-boundary result: under exact executable checkpoints and native carrier contracts, quantify the marginal benefit, carrier effect, and misinformation sensitivity of semantically valid E/S/A/V assistance on intent-valid repair. Source: fixed estimand, controls, and occupied-space audit.
- `[agent_inference]` This is more than a finer component ablation only if oracle-versus-untouched effects cannot be explained by sham activation, mismatched content, strong CodeTracer/Coherence-style process features, or a DoVer/FALAT-style replay baseline, and if native-contract and intent audits pass. Otherwise the direction collapses to component ablation and must be killed. Source: `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Baselines and statistical design; Three most likely top-venue rejection reasons`.
- `[agent_inference]` Versioned code checkpoints contribute a joint measurement structure: exact repository hashes, reversible executable actions, observation provenance, candidate-state validators, and behavioral outcomes can all be audited at one branch point. Source: scaffold schemas above; `synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P2, P5, P7`.
- `[agent_inference]` That structure is operationally stronger than an ordinary uninstrumented chat or web-agent trace, but it is not theoretically unique to software. A transactional simulator with equally versioned, forkable, executable state and independent intent oracles could substitute for it. The contribution is therefore code-agent evidence, not code exclusivity. Source: audit replay-infrastructure and cross-field boundaries.
- `[agent_inference]` A positive result would show only that one or more bounded external assistances have reproducible marginal utility, or reproducible harm under mismatch, at eligible executable checkpoints after carrier and leak controls. It would not show why the base agent failed. Source: estimand interpretation.
- `[agent_inference]` A robust negative result could show that native assistance effects are absent, interchangeable, dominated by carrier/authority artifacts, non-separable from mismatch, or no more informative than strong process/replay baselines. Such a result bounds this measurement strategy; a feasibility-only pilot does not by itself establish a general null. Source: audit positive/negative-result value.
