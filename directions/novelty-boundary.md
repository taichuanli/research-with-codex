- `[agent_inference]` Only `FINALIST_STAGEWISE_CAUSAL_RESCUE_PROFILES` remains. Its novelty is conditional empirical causal evidence, not a new causal method. Source: `directions/direction-narrowing-comparison.md / Stagewise failure attribution as a standalone paper`.

## Stagewise finalist: allowed claims

- `[agent_inference]` Allowed only after a positive pilot: observationally matched visible loop signatures can have different, reproducible causal rescue profiles over evidence, derived state, action, and validation interfaces. Source: finalist hypothesis and pilot gates.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed only after transfer evidence: the phenomenon appears in two independently implemented scaffolds, two model families, multiple repositories, and more than one task family. Source: `directions/minimum-kill-pilot.md / Minimum scale and transfer gate`.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed: versioned repositories and executable tools make stage intervention and off-target auditing unusually concrete, supporting stronger causal evidence than endpoint ablation alone. Source: stagewise code-structure assessment.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed: the study provides an empirical causal measurement protocol and artifact, provided it is described as an application of mature intervention machinery. Source: targeted causal-method works above.

## Stagewise finalist: prohibited claims

- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: first causal analysis of agent trajectories, first causal coding-agent audit, or absence of an exact prior study. Source: `AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims`; AuditRepairBench, Aegis, and sequential-causality coverage above.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: new interchange intervention, mediation estimand, actual-cause definition, credit-assignment algorithm, or generic causal attribution method. Source: Geiger et al., Ge et al., Triantafyllou et al., and Schug et al. above.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: a new failure taxonomy, recovery-window phenomenon, or general claim that visible loops are always non-identifying. Source: *Failure as a Process*, Aegis, and the finalist's scoped hypothesis.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: hidden-test success equals user intent, or a developer patch is the unique correct action. Source: `synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P7`; PatchDiff evidence cited there.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: the four stages are identified mediators merely because separate harness hooks exist. Isolation, consistency, and off-target checks must pass first. Source: dynamic mediation assumptions and pilot intervention-integrity gate.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: general-agent transfer based only on two code-agent runs. The claim is code-agent causal evidence with a possible broader implication, not a demonstrated universal law. Source: stagewise substitutability boundary.

## Memory direction: terminal boundary for this phase

- `[agent_inference]` A new retriever, compressor, relevance score, counterfactual utility score, provenance store, deletion policy, corruption detector, or action gate is outside the surviving space because direct and cross-field work covers those components. Source: memory direct ledger; targeted works above.
- `[agent_inference]` Snapshot-specific truth alone is not a sufficient novelty delta because belief update already time-indexes changed-world propositions and truth-maintenance systems already propagate dependency and retraction. Source: Hansson Section 6.1; Shapiro Sections 1, 3, and 6.
- `[agent_inference]` Dual endpoints of next action and hidden behavior strengthen measurement but do not create a new attribution principle; MPBench and MemLineage already connect corrupted persistent memory to later actions. Source: their locators above.
- `[agent_inference]` The direction may be reseeded only around a demonstrated program-semantic boundary, such as a causal interaction between executable invariants and memory updates that cannot be reproduced by another versioned transactional environment. This is a future pressure point, not a retained claim. Source: application-transfer decision and `AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims`.

## Merge boundary

- `[agent_inference]` A shared replay engine, intervention API, dataset, or benchmark does not unify research questions. A merge would be reconsidered only if one predeclared causal estimand necessarily contains both interface-level rescue and temporal item-level validity, with a single positive and negative interpretation. Source: current merge rejection.
- `[agent_inference]` The current pair fails that test: the stagewise hypothesis survives if memory attribution is ordinary, and the memory hypothesis could fail while the stagewise rescue phenomenon holds. Source: logical decomposition in `directions/direction-narrowing-comparison.md`.

## Evidence uncertainty

- `[direct_evidence]` AuditRepairBench, MPBench, and MemLineage are recent 2026 preprints or workshop-stage works, so version history and independent replication are immature. Source: arXiv version headers and MPBench venue line in the inspected PDFs.
- `[agent_inference]` Their recency limits confidence in their results but does not restore broad novelty claims already covered by mature causal, attribution, belief-update, and truth-maintenance traditions. Source: mature works above.
- `[agent_inference]` No missing exact-title result was treated as evidence of novelty. The finalist remains conditional on independent audit and a kill-first pilot. Source: `AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims`; current phase decision.
