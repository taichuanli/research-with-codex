## Why executable code matters, and its limit

- `[agent_inference]` A versioned repository provides a jointly auditable tuple of transcript/event state, repository hash, reversible executable action, resulting observation, candidate diff, and behavioral validator. This permits direct carrier-diff and semantic-validity checks unavailable in an ordinary uninstrumented conversation trace. Source: scaffold schemas in `directions/direction-revision.md / Scaffold-specific native mappings`; `synthesis/cross-paper-synthesis.md / P2, P5, P7`.
- `[agent_inference]` Code is not theoretically non-substitutable. Another domain with versioned, forkable, executable state and independent intent oracles could implement the same estimand. A paper must claim code-agent evidence and artifact auditability, not software-exclusive causality. Source: independent audit replay-infrastructure boundary and prior cross-field substitutability assessment.
- `[agent_inference]` If repository executability serves only as a convenient benchmark runner and does not validate treatment semantics, off-target changes, alternative valid actions, and final intent, the residual collapses to ordinary component ablation. Source: audit top rejection reasons.

## Allowed claims after evidence, not before

- `[agent_inference]` Allowed after a positive, adequately powered later study: at eligible exact code-agent checkpoints, a specified native assistance has a nonzero conditional total rescue effect relative to untouched continuation. Source: revised estimand.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed after sham and mismatch separation: the measured effect is associated with semantically valid content rather than only interface activation, authoritative form, token count, or misleading-control damage. Source: revised controls.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed after strong-baseline comparison: executable intervention adds a practical measurement result not recovered by the tested process-feature and replay baselines. Source: audit baseline requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` Allowed after observing a negative result with valid contracts: the tested native-carrier measurement has a bounded applicability or construct-validity failure on the eligible population. Source: revised kill conditions.

## Prohibited claims

- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: the interface with the largest rescue effect caused the original failure, is the endogenous causal bottleneck, is a mediator, or identifies root cause. Source: audit interface-identifiability judgment.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: a new failure taxonomy, failure-cause classifier, decisive-error definition, recovery-window phenomenon, intervention operator, causal estimator, checkpoint protocol, or replay system. Source: occupied-space audit.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: same visible loops imply different causes, or loop matching establishes causal comparability. Process features may be baselines only. Source: audit same-surface-loop judgment.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: hidden-test success equals user intent, the developer patch is the unique correct action, or an oracle is leak-free without frozen provenance and audit. Source: PatchDiff evidence and audit hidden-correctness warning.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: cross-scaffold, cross-model, cross-task, cross-language, general-agent, or universal transfer from the feasibility pilot. Source: revised scope and audit precision requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` Prohibited: "first", novel, or unprecedented based on the absence of an exact four-cell predecessor. Source: `AGENTS.md / Evidence and Claims`.

## Positive and negative result boundary

- `[agent_inference]` A positive result can establish marginal utility and misinformation asymmetry of bounded external assistance at eligible code-agent checkpoints. It cannot establish an internal explanation of failure. Source: estimand interpretation.
- `[agent_inference]` If sham effects match oracle effects, the result supports a carrier/authority artifact, not semantic rescue. If mismatched controls drive the ordering, the study measures susceptibility to misleading assistance. Source: control definitions.
- `[agent_inference]` If all four effects are practically indistinguishable or strong baselines reach the repeatability ceiling, the four-way interface matrix adds no result beyond finer component ablation and the direction is killed. Source: `directions/minimum-kill-pilot.md / KILL_FINE_ABLATION`.
- `[agent_inference]` If native carriers, leak-free payloads, or intent adjudication fail, no outcome can rescue the direction; it returns to idea seeding around reproducible evidence or intent validation rather than expanding the current frame. Source: requested hard-return rule and feasibility gates.
