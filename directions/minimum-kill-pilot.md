# Revised Minimum Falsifiability Boundary

## Status and phase boundary

- `[agent_inference]` This file supersedes the earlier full `Stagewise Causal Rescue Profiles` pilot plan. It defines only feasibility gates, kill conditions, and minimum data requirements; it is not a pilot design, implementation, task download, model call, training run, or experiment. Source: requested `DIRECTION_REVISION` boundary; `directions/direction-revision.md`.
- `[direct_evidence]` The independent audit required at least five independent continuations per retained condition and either at least 48 valid checkpoints across eight repositories for a transfer claim or removal of transfer claims and a feasibility-only pilot. Source: `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Required revisions before any experiment / 3`.
- `[agent_inference]` This revision takes the second option: the minimum pilot is feasibility-only, and no scaffold/model/task transfer claim is retained. Source: `directions/direction-revision.md / Claims removed or narrowed`.
- `[agent_inference]` `pilot_authorized` remains `false`; only an `INDEPENDENT_DIRECTION_REAUDIT` may decide whether later pilot design is warranted. Source: requested checkpoint rule.

## Fixed target

- `[agent_inference]` The only estimand is `tau_j(C_i)`, the probability of an intent-valid repair under a valid oracle substitution on native interface `j` minus the probability under untouched replay at the same restored checkpoint and fixed continuation policy. Source: `directions/direction-revision.md / Revised research question and unique estimand`.
- `[agent_inference]` Neutral sham and mismatched conditions are separate diagnostics relative to untouched replay. No oracle-minus-harmful contrast, dominant-stage label, bottleneck label, failure-cause label, or transfer conclusion is a pilot target. Source: `directions/direction-revision.md / Intervention conditions and primary contrasts; Claims removed or narrowed`.

## Feasibility gates before outcome interpretation

- `[agent_inference]` All gates are evaluated without using condition outcomes to repair payloads or replace checkpoints. Source: pre-outcome audit and selection-bias boundary.

1. `[agent_inference]` **F1, exact restoration:** At least 95% of screened checkpoints must reproduce the repository tree hash, candidate diff, deterministic public tool/test outputs, scaffold event prefix, process policy, and remaining budget on three dry restores. Below 90% kills checkpointed measurement; 90-95% permits no scientific interpretation and requires a fresh re-audit after one bounded engineering repair. Source: audit replay warning; prior E1 narrowed to feasibility.
2. `[agent_inference]` **F2, native contract conformance:** Both pinned scaffold classes must expose all four logical carriers under the matrix in `directions/direction-revision.md`, and at least 80% of otherwise replayable screened checkpoints must support all four without a study-only prompt slot. Any interface absent from either scaffold class, or overall support below 60%, kills the four-interface direction; 60-80% is inconclusive and returns to re-audit rather than selective replenishment. Source: audit absent/fused-interface rule.
3. `[agent_inference]` **F3, direct-write integrity:** At least 95% of pre-outcome branch audits must change exactly one declared logical carrier and only its serializer-coupled copies. Any unresolved structural invalidity, treatment-specific off-target rate above 5%, or field fusion that prevents E/V or S/A separation kills the affected contract. Source: audit interface-identifiability requirement.
4. `[agent_inference]` **F4, provenance and leak firewall:** Every retained oracle must have complete provenance and zero confirmed forbidden-source access. Any confirmed developer-patch, hidden-test-source, hidden-expected-output, future-suffix, or later-interface leak kills that payload; two confirmed leaks or any systemic leak path kill the interface. Unresolved suspected leakage is inconclusive, never clean. Source: `directions/direction-revision.md / Oracle provenance, leak audit, and intent adjudication`.
5. `[agent_inference]` **F5, comparator validity:** At least 90% of sham payloads must pass the task-agnostic schema audit, and at least 90% of mismatched payloads must be independently verified false or irrelevant and reversible before outcomes. Failure concentrated in one interface kills that control contract; accidentally valid mismatches are inconclusive. Source: revised control definitions.
6. `[agent_inference]` **F6, intent adjudication:** At least 90% of completed branches must receive a treatment-blind valid/invalid judgment under the multi-oracle and alternative-patch protocol. Overall adjudicability below 80%, a treatment gap above 10 percentage points, or unresolved systematic disagreement kills outcome measurement. Source: audit hidden-correctness requirement and PatchDiff uncertainty.
7. `[agent_inference]` **F7, comparison readiness:** Before any marginal-value interpretation, the records must support a CodeTracer/Coherence-style process-feature baseline, a DoVer/FALAT-style replay/intervention baseline, and native untouched continuation. If either strong baseline cannot be represented on the same checkpoint units, the pilot can report engineering feasibility only and cannot support the residual contribution. Source: `reports/independent-direction-audit-report.md / Baselines and statistical design`.

## Minimum data requirement

- `[agent_inference]` The feasibility floor is 16 fully eligible checkpoint units from at least four repositories and two independently implemented scaffold classes, with at least six units per scaffold and no more than one checkpoint per task. This floor tests whether the contract is more than an isolated fixture; it is explicitly too small for population heterogeneity or transfer inference. Source: audit nested-unit warning; current feasibility-only scope.
- `[agent_inference]` Every retained checkpoint must expose all four native contracts and all 13 conditions: one untouched replay plus neutral sham, mismatched, and oracle substitution for each of E/S/A/V. Selective omission of a difficult interface changes the direction and fails F2. Source: unique estimand and revised controls.
- `[agent_inference]` Each retained condition requires at least five independent post-checkpoint continuations under the same request configuration and budget. Cached responses may verify serialization but do not count as independent continuations. Source: audit repeat requirement and API nondeterminism warning.
- `[agent_inference]` The checkpoint, nested within task, repository, scaffold, and model configuration, is the analysis unit. Branches and repeats are within-unit measurements. Any interval must be hierarchical or repository/task clustered, and sensitivity must include practical-effect thresholds of 0.05, 0.10, and 0.20 rather than relying on the old 0.33 dominance rule. Source: audit statistical-design requirement.
- `[agent_inference]` A future cross-scaffold/model/task transfer claim is outside this feasibility pilot. It requires a separately approved design with at least 48 valid checkpoints across eight repositories, balanced nested cells, and the same five-repeat minimum; those numbers are not permission to begin that study. Source: audit required revision / 3.

## Direction-kill and inconclusive conditions

- `[agent_inference]` **KILL_CONTRACT:** Triggered by F2 or F3 failure. Conclude that a comparable native four-interface measurement cannot be implemented; do not replace missing contracts with generic prompts or drop an interface after outcomes. Source: audit pre-experiment blocker.
- `[agent_inference]` **KILL_LEAK_OR_INTENT:** Triggered by systemic F4 or F6 failure. Conclude that leak-free assistance or intent-valid outcomes cannot be established; return to the relevant idea pressure point rather than expanding oracle construction. Source: P7 and requested hard-return rule.
- `[agent_inference]` **KILL_CHANNEL_ARTIFACT:** Triggered when oracle effects are not directionally larger than the corresponding sham effects at any interface and sham activation accounts for any apparent ordering under all predeclared sensitivity thresholds. Conclude that carrier/authority activation, not validated content, explains the result. Source: audit placebo warning.
- `[agent_inference]` **KILL_NO_MARGINAL_RESCUE:** Triggered when the hierarchical 90% upper interval for every oracle-versus-untouched `tau_j` is below 0.10. Conclude that no practically useful marginal rescue signal is present in the eligible feasibility population. Source: fixed estimand and 0.10 sensitivity threshold.
- `[agent_inference]` **KILL_FINE_ABLATION:** Triggered when strong process and replay baselines reproduce the same checkpoint-level practical conclusion within the repeatability ceiling, or when E/S/A/V oracle effects cannot be distinguished beyond the 0.10 sensitivity threshold. Conclude that the four-way matrix adds no empirical insight beyond finer component ablation. Source: audit prior-art and baseline rejection reason.
- `[agent_inference]` **INCONCLUSIVE:** Use when the 16-unit floor is reached but uncertainty spans both no effect and practical rescue, or when a non-systemic bounded number of branches fail provenance, control, or outcome adjudication. Inconclusive results do not authorize threshold changes, selective new checkpoints, transfer claims, or a full study. Source: evidence discipline and kill-first boundary.
- `[agent_inference]` **FEASIBILITY_ONLY_PASS:** Requires F1-F7, no kill condition, and complete reporting of untouched, sham, mismatched, and oracle contrasts plus exclusions. It establishes only that a later design is technically and constructively testable; it does not establish contribution, novelty, causation, or transfer. Source: current phase boundary.

## Positive and negative meaning

- `[agent_inference]` A positive feasibility signal means that semantically validated external assistance can have marginal value beyond carrier activation at eligible executable checkpoints. It does not identify an endogenous failure cause or causal bottleneck. Source: revised estimand.
- `[agent_inference]` A negative feasibility signal can bound native-carrier availability, leak-free intervention, intent adjudication, marginal assistance value, or added value over strong baselines. The bound applies only to the eligible checkpoint population and is not a universal agent null. Source: audit positive/negative-result value.
